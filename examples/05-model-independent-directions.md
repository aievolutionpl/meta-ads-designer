# Three advertising prompts with different design decisions

These are fictional teaching briefs with explicitly supplied facts. No images were generated or scored. The prompts demonstrate design reasoning, not measured campaign performance.

## 1 · A typographic event flyer

Brief: fictional event “NOC BRZMI”, 18 October 2026, 20:00, Studio 8, tickets at nocbrzmi.example. The organiser supplied all details and requests a bold Polish social flyer. No logo supplied.

Decision: the event name is the hero. A single rhythmic graphic suggests sound; there is no reason to invent a venue photograph.

Finished-ad prompt:

> Create one vertical 4:5 social event flyer. Use a flat graphic composition with a cobalt background, warm-white lettering and one acid-yellow sound-wave shape. The reading order is event name, date and time, venue, ticket destination. Put “NOC” and “BRZMI” on two oversized, left-aligned lines in the upper half. Use a heavy condensed grotesk display treatment, similar in width and weight to Anton, with solid unbroken letterforms. Below, group “18.10.2026 · 20:00” and “Studio 8” in a clear medium-weight sans serif. Place “Bilety: nocbrzmi.example” near the bottom inside generous margins. Keep all information aligned to the headline's left edge. One broad yellow wave enters from the right edge behind the empty space beside the title; it does not cross letters. Leave a quiet gap between the title and event details. No photographs, extra badges, logos, small icons or additional text. Make the event name dominant while keeping the date and ticket line legible on a phone.

Production note: .example is an illustrative reserved domain, not a real ticket destination. Replace the supplied facts for a real event. Verify Polish lettering after rendering; typeset separately if fidelity fails.

## 2 · A food ad with an honest source image

Brief: fictional cafe “PORA”; a croissant photograph is supplied as image A. Goal: breakfast visits. Approved copy: “Poranek ma warstwy.” and “Wpadnij na śniadanie”. No approved price or opening hours.

Decision: visible pastry layers support the headline. Warm colour comes from the actual pastry, not invented luxury decor. Exact Polish copy will be typeset separately.

Image prompt:

> Create one photographic background for a vertical 4:5 breakfast advertisement. Image A is the exact pastry reference: preserve its shape, visible layers, colour and serving presentation. Place that croissant large in the lower-right area on a simple off-white table, with the entire pastry inside the frame. Photograph from a close three-quarter angle so the flaky side faces the viewer. Soft morning light from the upper left reveals the layers, with a gentle contact shadow extending right. Keep the upper-left third calm and almost empty for a headline. Use warm natural pastry tones against a quiet neutral setting; focus on the front layers and let the far table soften. Do not add other food, packaging, signage, text, logos or props that imply an unconfirmed breakfast bundle.

Typesetting handoff: preferred Archivo Bold headline at upper left, “Poranek ma” / “warstwy.” on two lines; Source Sans 3 for “Wpadnij na śniadanie” below. Charcoal type. Set “PORA” as an approved brand-name text cue, not a fabricated official logo. Check the real crop and font support before export.

## 3 · A local service with credible proof

Brief: fictional bicycle workshop “KOŁO”; supplied image A shows the actual mechanic adjusting a bicycle. Goal: service enquiries. Approved copy: “Rower gotowy na trasę.” and “Umów przegląd”. No promised turnaround or savings.

Decision: the real action provides credibility. Use a documentary visual and restrained copy rather than a fictitious before/after result.

Image-edit prompt:

> Prepare image A as the photographic field of one vertical 4:5 bicycle-service ad. Preserve the actual mechanic's identity, hands, tools, bicycle geometry and workshop details. Keep the adjustment action as the dominant focal area in the middle-right of the composition. Retain the documentary character and existing light direction; gently balance exposure so the hand-tool contact is clear without metallic glare. Plan a calm dark text area along the left side using a separate solid design field if the original crop has no room. Do not redraw the action to manufacture empty space. No new tools, replacement parts, uniforms, testimonials, badges or lettering. Preserve natural skin and metal surfaces.

Typesetting handoff: left-aligned white Source Sans 3 Bold headline, “Rower gotowy” / “na trasę.”; smaller “Umów przegląd” below; approved text cue “KOŁO” above. Use a single orange rule to separate brand cue from headline. If the source cannot support the proposed side field, use a photo-above/type-below layout while preserving the action. No invented time guarantee.

## 4 · Why these are different

The event uses type as the hero, the cafe uses a tactile detail, and the workshop uses an authentic action. Their hierarchy follows their marketing job. None relies on naming an image model or adding “premium, cinematic, ultra-detailed”.
