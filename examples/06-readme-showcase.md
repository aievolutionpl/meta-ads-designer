# README showcase: art direction in three concepts

This is an AI-generated editorial presentation board made for this repository. DAYBREAK, AFTER HOURS and FORM are fictional demonstration identities. The board is not a real campaign, a controlled before/after experiment or evidence of conversion improvement. It is one presentation image containing three illustrative ad directions, not three separately exported production ads.

The food concept uses a photographed-looking subject and restrained serif typography. The event uses condensed type as the hero. The product concept uses silhouette, material and shadow. The shared editorial grid makes the choices easy to compare.

## Production

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool. No client assets or external brand logos were used. The skill itself remains model-independent. The previous burger banner was treated as a rejected direction, not an identity reference.

## Exact generation prompt

```text
Create a polished professional editorial portfolio cover for the open-source project META ADS DESIGNER. Landscape 16:9, high resolution. This is one cohesive graphic design presentation board, not a software dashboard. Warm white paper background, near-black typography, precise Swiss editorial alignment, ample whitespace. Top left very large bold clean grotesk title on two lines: "META ADS" then "DESIGNER". Small subtitle beneath: "Art direction for image prompts." At top right small text "BRIEF / COMPOSITION / PROMPT".
The lower two thirds contain three equally sized beautifully art-directed portrait advertising examples in a perfectly aligned row with generous white gutters. Each miniature ad is fully flat/front-on, no perspective mockup, no device frames.
Left example: natural editorial food photography, one real-looking croissant on a white ceramic plate on a terracotta cafe table, warm sidelight, believable flaky layers but restrained detail, no floating ingredients. Upper uncluttered ivory area with refined dark serif headline "Slow mornings." and small fictional cafe brand "DAYBREAK". Image feels photographed for an independent bakery.
Middle example: striking typographic music-event poster, saturated cobalt blue background, huge warm-white condensed lettering "AFTER" and "HOURS" in two lines, a single broad lime curved graphic flowing beside the letters, tiny but clean secondary line "LIVE SESSIONS". No photography. Confident experimental print-design taste, highly legible.
Right example: refined contemporary product ad, one sculptural matte brick-red ceramic cup resting firmly on a pale stone surface, architectural soft sunlight from left and believable diagonal shadow. Large negative space. Restrained dark sans headline "Everyday form." near top, small fictional brand "FORM". No invented logo marks.
Below each portrait on the white board, simple tiny labels "01 / FOOD", "02 / CULTURE", "03 / PRODUCT". Keep all type correctly spelled and margins generous. Overall looks like a respected independent design studio's portfolio case study, quiet, tactile, credible, thoughtfully art-directed. NO neon, holograms, techno UI, model names, tool logos, exploded objects, floating particles, gradients, glowing edges, performance claims, badges, arrows, or decorative icons. The composition itself demonstrates variety and hierarchy.
```

## Style Atlas 2026 board

[Style Atlas 2026](../assets/style-atlas-2026.png) is not an AI-generated image. It is typeset deterministically in HTML and CSS from [its source](../assets/generated/style-atlas.html) and rendered with a headless browser, which is the Mode B route the skill recommends when copy, diacritics and layout must be exact. The product drawings are flat vector illustrations, not photographs.

Every brand on it is fictional: NOC BRZMI and its reserved `.example` domain come from [example 05](05-model-independent-directions.md); NURT, PORZĄDEK and LUMA from [example 07](07-2026-style-directions.md); TARG WINYLI and ZIARNO exist only on the board. The number 47/60, the offer and the comparison rows are teaching content, not claims about any real product. The board demonstrates format and style decisions; it is not evidence of advertising performance.

To re-render after editing the HTML, open it in a Chromium-based browser at a 1400×1420 viewport and capture at 1.5× device scale.
