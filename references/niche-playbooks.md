## 🍽️ Restaurant / food / café

### What works
- **Real food wins.** If the client has dish photos, they are the hero. Never let AI invent dishes the venue doesn't serve.
- **Real photo + solid panel:** photo on top (~62%) + a solid dark or brand-coloured panel below (~38%) carrying headline/subline/CTA/logo. Hard separation, zero text on the food. (layout-system §3a)
- **Dark studio editorial** (black background, single spotlight, medium format) — only when the client has no usable photos and accepts stylization.
- **Native in-scene text** when the model spells reliably: small serif brand at the top, big bold headline, one short location line.
- **"Food in use"** — the dish on a table, natural light, bokeh — beats a flat overhead on white.
- **Café/coffee specifically:** steam, natural window light, the cup in a hand or on a counter — sensory warmth, not a stock "cappuccino on marble".

### What to avoid
- ❌ **Text-on-photo Canva template** — the #1 rejection.
- ❌ AI-gourmet: fine-dining plating for a casual venue.
- ❌ A landscape table-spread force-cropped to 4:5 with the plates clipped.
- ❌ Beverages: misspelled cup branding, or a logo re-drawn onto the cup.

### Headline archetypes that work here
CONCRETE (name the dish) · SENSORY · PLACE · DEADLINE (midweek offers)

### Typical CTA
`Reserve a table` · `Order now` · `See the menu`

## 🏨 Hotel / venue / accommodation

### What works
- **Coastal / travel-magazine editorial:** golden hour, real lens language (e.g. 24–70mm f/2.8 at the wide end).
- **The real facade as hero**, then a direct-booking CTA.
- Structurally different layouts: heritage poster · seaside escape · terrace/dining · events · direct-booking offer · arrival/evening.
- Negative space as luxury. Controlled shadows, premium grade.
- **The editorial split** (photo as a placed block on a flat brand field) is the fastest way out of generic hospitality.

### What to avoid
- ❌ AI drawing the logo onto the building — place the original file deterministically.
- ❌ Re-generating a listed or distinctive facade — it invents balconies and windows.
- ❌ Too many small text rows in the footer — it has to read from a thumbnail.

### Headline archetypes that work here
OBJECTION (the portal-is-cheaper belief) · NUMBER (the direct-booking gap) · PLACE · PROOF

### Typical CTA
`Book direct` · `Check availability` · `See the rooms`

## 🏪 Local business / services / home improvement

### What works
- **Real product/install photos as references** → generate **new** premium scenes (different light, time of day, lifestyle). Never overlay on the client's raw photo.
- **Angles that sell services:** Problem → Effect (before/after when it proves value instantly) · package tiers ("Installation included", "£0 deposit") · genuine deadline offers ("Fitted before October 31") · transformation (chaos → order, dark → light).
- **Benefit-led headline ≤40 characters**, body ≤125.
- Readable CTA + logo fidelity + location/phone — all deterministic, never AI-rendered.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Pasting frames or gradients onto the client's raw photos.
- ❌ AI mangling a product brand or a piece of equipment.
- ❌ Invented urgency. If there's no real cut-off, use PROOF or OBJECTION instead.
- ❌ Before/after inside one frame — it halves both halves and fails the thumbnail test. Ship a pair.

### Headline archetypes that work here
CONTRAST · DEADLINE · PROOF · OBJECTION

### Typical CTA
`Book a survey` · `Call today` · `See our work`

## 🛍️ Retail / product / e-commerce

### What works
- **Product in use** in a real context — not floating on a gradient.
- Real people, real environments, real light.
- Studio hero: clean background, directional light, product as hero, zero clutter.
- For anything mechanical: supply **two** references — the packshot for appearance, a drawing for how it moves.
- **E-commerce:** the product sharp and 100% recognisable; scale and texture visible from a thumbnail; colour true to the listing (returns are killed by mismatched colour).

### What to avoid
- ❌ Floating product on a gradient with a glow — the most common product slop.
- ❌ Isometric illustration for a physical product.
- ❌ Drifting counts across a series (tiers, buttons, fixings) — pin them explicitly.

### Headline archetypes that work here
CONCRETE · AUDIENCE · NUMBER · CONTRAST

### Typical CTA
`Shop now` · `See the collection` · `Order today`

## 🎉 Event / nightlife / promotion

### What works
- Strong typography; a big title readable from a thumbnail.
- Event rows (date/venue) legible, not crowded.
- One hero + date/venue/CTA. Nothing else.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Overloading the frame — too much information means nothing gets read.
- ❌ More than one accent colour; poster design fails fast on colour noise.

### Headline archetypes that work here
DEADLINE · PLACE · AUDIENCE

### Typical CTA
`Get tickets` · `Reserve your spot`

## 💪 Fitness / gym / coaching

### What works
- **Real bodies, real effort, real sweat** — not airbrushed CGI. Progress, not perfection.
- **Transformation as a series** — the pair/sequence proves the outcome (ship as separate frames, never split-screen).
- **Before/after or day-1 → today** storytelling when the client has real photos.
- Strong energetic light (window light, gym natural light); motion and grit over studio shine.
- A confident, direct headline + one clear offer (free class, consultation, trial week).

### What to avoid
- ❌ Perfect unreal bodies — looks fake, kills trust.
- ❌ Harsh neon gym-lighting clichés and chrome.
- ❌ Invented results or fake testimonials.

### Headline archetypes that work here
AUDIENCE · COMMAND · CONTRAST · PROOF

### Typical CTA
`Start free` · `Book a trial` · `Join the gym`

## ✨ Beauty / cosmetics / spa

### What works
- **Editorial beauty photography** — soft light, flawless but believable skin, elegant negative space.
- **The product as hero** next to a single model or in a clean composition; the bottle/jar 100% recognisable.
- Pastel or brand-palette backgrounds; premium, minimal, spa-calm.
- Real ingredient/result callouts as small, legible type — never slop on the product.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Fake glow, plastic skin, or "AI beauty filter" faces.
- ❌ Redrawing the product label or brand name.
- ❌ Too much copy — beauty sells on feeling + one proof point.

### Headline archetypes that work here
SENSORY · PROOF · AUDIENCE · CONTRAST

### Typical CTA
`Shop the range` · `Book a treatment` · `Discover`

## 🏠 Real estate / property

### What works
- **The real property is the hero** — supply real photos, never let AI invent architecture.
- **Golden hour exterior + clean interior** shots; bright, aspirational, wide-angle-real (not distorted CGI).
- Key facts as clean type: price, location, beds/baths, agent — readable from a thumbnail.
- Consistent light across the series (all golden hour or all daylight) for a premium feel.

### What to avoid
- ❌ AI-generated dream homes that don't exist — the buyer will never see that.
- ❌ Distorted wide-angle or fake sky replacements.
- ❌ Small unreadable fine print. Location + price must pop.

### Headline archetypes that work here
NUMBER · PLACE · AUDIENCE · DEADLINE (open houses)

### Typical CTA
`Book a viewing` · `See the listing` · `Contact agent`

## 🚗 Automotive / dealership

### What works
- **The real vehicle as hero** — clean studio or dramatic location, correct proportions.
- Motion/aspiration: on the road, golden hour, cinematic — but the car stays recognisable and true to colour.
- Spec highlights as clean type (year, mileage, price) — not on the car.
- Dealer identity + contact deterministic, never AI-rendered onto the paint.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Wrong body proportions, extra doors, fake badging, misdrawn wheels.
- ❌ "Dream car" CGI that isn't the actual stock.
- ❌ Text or logo re-drawn onto the vehicle.

### Headline archetypes that work here
NUMBER · AUDIENCE · DEADLINE · CONTRAST

### Typical CTA
`Book a test drive` · `View stock` · `Get a quote`

## 🧠 Education / courses / training

### What works
- **The outcome, not the classroom** — a graduate working, a certificate held, a skill in use.
- Clean, trustworthy, no gimmick: real people, real results, clear next step.
- Structure aids: roadmap visuals, "module 1→5" as clean graphics (deterministic).
- Proof: placement, results, reviews — stated, never invented.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Fake diplomas, invented success stories, or stock "student smiling at laptop" clichés.
- ❌ Overcrowded infographic-style frames.

### Headline archetypes that work here
AUDIENCE · PROOF · NUMBER · OBJECTION

### Typical CTA
`Enrol now` · `Start learning` · `Get the syllabus`

## 🩺 Health / clinic / supplements / wellness

### What works
- **Trust and calm** — clean, clinical-but-warm; real facilities or real products.
- One clear benefit per ad (not a list of 10 claims).
- Real dosage/label details deterministic — a supplement label is never AI-rendered.
- Proof (clinics, certifications, results) stated responsibly.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Unrealistic bodies or miracle claims — risky and fake.
- ❌ Redrawing medicine/supplement packaging or warnings.
- ❌ Fear-based slop; keep it professional and reassuring.

### Headline archetypes that work here
OBJECTION · PROOF · AUDIENCE · NUMBER

### Typical CTA
`Book a consultation` · `Speak to us` · `Learn more`

## 💼 Finance / professional services (accounting, law, insurance, consulting)

### What works
- **Credibility over flash** — clean layouts, strong typography, a calm brand field.
- The human/professional + a concrete promise (tax saved, claim won, coverage found).
- Numbers do the selling: "£X saved", "N clients", "response in 24h" — real, verifiable.
- Deterministic composition (Mode B) — precision and legal-safety of text matter.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Generic stock "handshake + skyscraper" imagery.
- ❌ Overclaiming or invented figures.
- ❌ Dense fine print that can't be read — either real and legible or not there.

### Headline archetypes that work here
NUMBER · OBJECTION · PROOF · AUDIENCE

### Typical CTA
`Book a free call` · `Get a quote` · `Talk to an advisor`

## 💻 Tech / SaaS / app

### What works
- **The product UI is the hero** — a real, recognisable interface shot (never invented UI), or the output it produces.
- Clean, modern, whitespace-led design; one feature per ad.
- Abstract enough to feel premium, concrete enough to show value.
- The user's win in words, not just the logo: "Save 4 hours a week".

### What to avoid
- ❌ Fake/fictional UI screens — inventing screens is a hard fail (user will never see that interface).
- ❌ HUD/cyberpunk clichés, neon grids, or "futuristic" noise.
- ❌ Text/UI re-drawn by the model — use real screenshots (Mode B).

### Headline archetypes that work here
NUMBER · AUDIENCE · CONTRAST · OBJECTION

### Typical CTA
`Start free` · `Book a demo` · `Download the app`

## 👗 Fashion / apparel

### What works
- **Real garments on real bodies**, editorial styling, natural or studio light.
- Fabric, drape and texture must read — sell the material, not a mood.
- Consistent model/brand identity across the series.
- Clean backgrounds let the outfit own the frame.

### What to avoid
- ❌ AI-redrawn logos, wrong button counts, misshapen garments.
- ❌ Skin retouched into plastic; keeping it believable builds desire.
- ❌ Silhouette-only shots that hide the actual product.

### Headline archetypes that work here
AUDIENCE · SENSORY · CONCRETE · DEADLINE (sales)

### Typical CTA
`Shop the look` · `View the collection` · `Buy now`

## 🐾 Pets / veterinary / pet care

- Services: happy pet + a clear offer (check-up, grooming, boarding).
- Product: the pet enjoying it (feeding, playing) — not a floating bag.
- Cute, but professional — never over-twee.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Misshapen animals or wrong breed features.
- ❌ Redrawing product packaging or vet credentials.

### Headline archetypes that work here
AUDIENCE · SENSORY · PLACE · PROOF

### Typical CTA

## 📸 Creative services / photography / design / agencies

### What works
- **Show the work, not the tools** — finished results, real portfolio shots.
- Editorial, gallery-like presentation; the craft is the product.
- Strong signature typography; the studio's own voice.
- A clear niche statement ("commercial food photography") + a CTA.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Stock "camera + laptop on desk" clichés.
- ❌ Generic agency-speak; let the portfolio prove the skill.

### Headline archetypes that work here
CONCRETE · PROOF · AUDIENCE · OBJECTION

### Typical CTA
`See the portfolio` · `Book a shoot` · `Start a project`

- Real practitioners/spaces, not generic "meditating silhouette".
- One promise per ad (better sleep, less stress, a reset).
- Soft, earthy or brand-palette colours; premium, quiet typography.

### What to avoid
- ❌ Neon "zen" clichés, fake aura/gradient glows.
- ❌ Overpromising therapeutic claims.

## 🧭 Universal: niche research in 30 seconds

1. **Meta Ad Library** → search the industry/location → look at 10 top ads.
2. **Instagram** → competitor profiles → note which layouts and colours repeat.
3. **Ask the client:** "do you have existing ads you like?" If yes, **that is the style source of truth.** Elevate their look; don't substitute your own generic "premium".
4. Note the niche's clichés — and treat them as the list of things your ad must not do.
